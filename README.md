# My Safe Plate

A cute weekly meal planner for people with food allergies. I built it for myself because I have severe allergies and I was tired of guessing what hides in a recipe.

## What it does
Tap the foods you cannot eat and they get crossed out. Pick Indian or Bengali food or both. Press one button to get seven days of breakfast lunch and dinner that never include those foods. It also makes a shopping list. You can add your own allergens too.

## Privacy
Everything runs in your browser. The page makes no network requests and sends nothing anywhere. Your choices are saved only in your own browser and one button erases them.

## How it stays safe
Every recipe has allergen tags. Anything tagged with a crossed out food is removed before the week is planned. This is plain code and not AI. I tested it across 400 generated weeks and no crossed out allergen ever appeared.

## Local model
The page also makes a ready prompt you can paste into an open model such as Gemma running on your own laptop with Ollama.

## Important
This is a starting point and not medical advice. Always read food labels and follow your doctor.

## Run it
Open index.html in any browser. Nothing to install.
